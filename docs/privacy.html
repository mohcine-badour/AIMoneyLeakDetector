Kichta Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Kichta tracks subscriptions on this device. We collect as little as we can and do not send receipts or money amounts to analytics. This page matches the in-app Privacy Policy.

On this device

Subscriptions, trials, usage counts, and onboarding answers stay in an encrypted local database on your phone. The encryption key is stored in the system Keychain, not in SharedPreferences. They are used to calculate spend, upcoming payments, and reminders.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for bank logins. We do not create a Kichta account. We do not keep raw receipt images after a scan. We do not put financial amounts, merchant names, or documents into analytics. The camera and photo library are used only to scan a receipt. Text is read on this phone. The image is held in memory for that scan, then discarded. Receipt files are not saved in the app database.

How the app works

Product analytics may record event names such as app opened or paywall viewed. Allowed details are limited to plan, source, success, and reminder kind. Amounts, merchants, and documents are stripped before anything is recorded. This build logs those event names on the device only.

Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple validates the purchase. If a RevenueCat public SDK key is configured later, RevenueCat may also see store account and product identifiers to restore entitlement. This app never stores an AI API key or a RevenueCat secret key.

Free users may see a small banner on Trials and Upcoming. Ads are served by Google AdMob and requested as non-personalized. We do not send amounts, merchant names, or receipts to the ad SDK. Pro removes ads. Home, add, scan, paywall, and Settings never show ads. Spend math stays in the app. This version does not send your data to an AI provider.

Your control

This version does not upload your subscription list to a Kichta account. Outbound links use HTTPS only.

You can delete the data on this device in Settings. There is no cloud account, so deletion is local only. App Store subscriptions are billed by Apple and are not cancelled by deleting app data.

Contact: the Support row in Settings, which opens GitHub issues, or App Store support for this app. Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are also available in the app.
